Keyboard shortcuts

EnterNew line. Moves on to the next element (Location to Action, Speaker to Dialogue). On an empty line it turns into Action.
TabOn an empty line: Action becomes Speaker, Dialogue becomes Note. Otherwise cycles the element. Shift+Tab goes back.
Ctrl+1 to 6Location, Action, Speaker, Note, Dialogue, Scene change
INT. or EXT.Typed in an Action line, it becomes a Location. Pick from the list, then a place, then the time of day.
Up, Down, TabChoose and accept a suggestion (names, places, times, V.O., O.S.)
Ctrl+B, I, UBold, italic, underline
[[ note ]]A note to yourself. It never prints unless you switch Print notes on.
Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Shift+ZUndo, redo
Ctrl+F, F3Find and replace, find next
Ctrl+KCommand palette
Ctrl+SSave now
Ctrl+/ or F1This list
EscClose a list, panel or the index cards
